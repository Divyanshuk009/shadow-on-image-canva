# Privacy Policy for Shadow on Image

**Effective Date:** November 2026

Shadow on Image ("the App") is operated by Divyanshu Kumar.

## 1. Data Processing & Storage
- All image rendering, shadow generation, and canvas effects run **locally in your web browser**.
- The App does **not** upload, transmit, or store your images or Canva designs on any external third-party servers.
- When you insert an image with a shadow back into your design, it is uploaded directly to your own private Canva media library using Canva's official SDK.
